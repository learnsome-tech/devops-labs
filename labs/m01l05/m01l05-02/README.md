## Goal

You can explain why almost every article names four DORA metrics, what changed in 2023 and 2024, and why mean time to restore was replaced rather than renamed.

In the lesson: The short history is on screen. The inaugural study looked at four variables, but change fail rate did not correlate with the others well enough to join a single measure of performance, so the first definition leaned on three. The year after, the model settled into a duality: throughput and stability. In twenty eighteen the outcome was renamed from information technology performance to software delivery and operational performance. In twenty twenty one reliability joined, and DORA has since said publicly that calling it the fifth metric was inaccurate, because reliability is an operational measure rather than a delivery one.
